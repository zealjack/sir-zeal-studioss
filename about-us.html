About Sir Zeal

I built the skills. Then I asked a harder question.

15+Clients in Year 1

I did everything the traditional path asked of me — learned to build, took the offer, signed the contract. Then one question wouldn't leave me alone.

"Is this really how I want to spend my life?"

So I started building for myself instead — and for businesses that needed the same thing I did: a system, not just a website. Most businesses don't fail from lack of effort. They fail because nothing is connected.

A website that exists but converts nobody. Social presence with no strategy behind it. Content going out with nothing to sell it into. No system tying discovery, trust, and payment together. I saw the same gap everywhere I looked, and I built Sir Zeal to close it — one business at a time.

Today that means three things: building the systems businesses need to get found, trusted, and paid; building Sir Zeal Academy to teach the skills that make that possible; and staying hands-on with every project instead of handing it off.

Why we build

Systems over noise. Substance over pretty. Results over promises.

How we operate

What every project is built on.

Honesty over hype

If something won't move the needle for your business, we'll tell you before we build it — not after you've paid for it.

Logic over fluff

Every design decision has a reason tied to conversion, clarity, or speed. Nothing goes in just because it looks nice.

Ownership, not dependency

You get clean, documented systems you actually own — not something that quietly breaks the moment we're not around.
